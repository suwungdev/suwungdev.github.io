Notes · 30 August 2026

Building a small project space.

Suwung.id started as a simple place to publish AIODL and document the process behind it. As more tools were built, the site evolved into a small home for projects that are useful, experimental, or simply worth keeping.

From one project to several

AIODL was the first public project under the suwung.id name. MYIP followed as an IP intelligence and network lookup tool, while QRIS Dinamis became a focused utility for generating dynamic payment QR codes from supported QRIS data.

Why keep the site simple?

A project portfolio does not need to behave like a large application. Keeping the landing pages static makes them fast, easy to deploy, and easy to maintain. The individual projects can then use whatever stack fits their own requirements.

What comes next

More tools and experiments may be added over time. The focus remains on useful features, understandable interfaces, stability, and documenting what was learned along the way.

Project note: Suwung.id is an independent project space. Individual projects may have their own repositories, technologies, terms, and limitations.
